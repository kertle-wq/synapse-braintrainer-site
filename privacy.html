Privacy Policy

Synapse: Braintrainer · Last updated: 2 October 2026

Synapse: Braintrainer ("Synapse", "the app") is a brain-training game app. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

Information stored only on your device

The app saves the following on your phone so it can work. It is not sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Your display name
  • Your onboarding choices (focus areas, goal and daily training minutes)
  • Your streak and game history

Deleting the app removes this data.

Information sent to our server (leaderboard)

Each time you finish a game, the app sends a small record to our leaderboard database so you can compete with other players:

  • Your display name
  • Which game you played
  • The calendar month
  • The time the session was logged

Your display name and session counts are visible to other players on the leaderboard, so please do not use your real name or any personal information as your display name.

This data is stored with Supabase (supabase.com), our database provider, which processes it on our behalf.

What we do not collect

  • No email address, phone number, password or account of any kind
  • No location, contacts, photos or microphone/camera data
  • No advertising identifiers, and no analytics or tracking tools
  • We do not sell or share your information for advertising

Retention and deletion

Leaderboard records are kept so monthly and historical rankings work. To have records linked to your display name deleted, contact us at the address below with the display name you used. Because there are no accounts, we can only match records by display name.

Children

Synapse is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
